PLANT HEALTH

What to watch

  • Soft or mushy areas: may indicate overwatering or rot.
  • Shrivelling: check the soil before adding water.
  • Discolouration or pests: photograph and investigate.
  • Slow growth: can be normal, especially in cooler months.
